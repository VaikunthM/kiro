ClearPick

A picking robot that makes space before it grabs. Both robots clear the same simulated totes. Grab-only always lifts the top item. ClearPick scores every grasp first and pushes crowded items apart.

Simulated in MuJoCo with simplified suction and pinch rules, so the numbers show how the two strategies compare, not how a real warehouse performs. The failure types come from real failed picks in Amazon's ARMBench; see the failure atlas.
