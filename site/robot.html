ClearPick

A picking robot that makes space before it grabs. Both robots work the same simulated totes: Grab-only always lifts the top item, ClearPick scores every grasp first and pushes crowded items apart.

Simulated in MuJoCo with simplified suction and pinch rules, so the numbers show how the two strategies compare, not how a real warehouse performs. Real failure photos from Amazon's ARMBench are on the failure atlas page.
